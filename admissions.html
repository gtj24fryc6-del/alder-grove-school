Who we support

Places at Alder Grove are for young people aged 11 to 16 who have an Education, Health and Care Plan (EHCP) that names Alder Grove, and whose primary identified need is autism. The school's current admissions policy states that it does not make provision for young people whose primary need is a physical disability.

Important

Placement decisions are made through the local authority EHCP process. Please discuss a possible placement with your local authority and contact the school if you need more information.

How the process works

  1. Your local authority consults Alder Grove and shares the EHCP, latest annual review, relevant professional reports and family views.
  2. The school reviews the information and responds within the statutory timescale, normally 15 days.
  3. If a placement is agreed, we work with the young person, family, local authority and relevant professionals to plan a supported transition.

To discuss a place, contact Headteacher Arshad Ashraf on 07587 060525 or use the contact page. The school email address needs to be confirmed before launch.

Starting at Alder Grove

Every pupil has a structured induction with transition planning, gradual integration where needed, and key staff allocated to them. In the first two weeks, initial risk assessments and behaviour support plans are put in place and baseline assessments begin. A review with the family, local authority and other professionals is planned within 6 to 12 weeks.
