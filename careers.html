Safeguarding and equal opportunities

Alder Grove School is committed to safeguarding and promoting the welfare of children and young people, and expects all staff to share that commitment. We are an equal opportunities employer and welcome applications from all sections of the community. Roles are based at 49 Bradford Street, Bolton, BL2 1HT.

How to apply

Applicants will need to complete the school application form in full; CVs cannot be accepted in place of the form. Applications should be sent to the recruitment email address by the stated closing date.

Offers are conditional on safer recruitment checks, including identity and right to work, an enhanced DBS check (with barred-list check where relevant), qualification checks, two references and, for shortlisted candidates, an online search. Applicants must declare convictions and cautions that are not protected.

New staff complete a structured induction, including safeguarding, first-aid awareness and school procedures, before working with pupils. Ongoing training includes autism, trauma-informed practice, behaviour support and safeguarding.
