Key dates & school day

The planned school day runs from 8.30am to 2.30pm, and registers close at 9.00am. All pupils are offered breakfast on arrival. Pupils hand in phones and other devices when they arrive and receive them back at the end of the day; any exceptions are individually risk-assessed and agreed with families.

To confirm before launch: term dates, INSET days, uniform and transport information.

Attendance

Please contact the school before 9.00am on each day your child is absent. If we have not heard from you by 9.15am, we will contact you. We work with families to understand and remove barriers to attendance. See the Attendance Policy when published.

Starting at Alder Grove

Each pupil has a structured induction, key staff, early assessments and a review within 6 to 12 weeks. Transition planning and gradual integration are arranged where needed, in partnership with the pupil, family and professionals.

Health & wellbeing

Pupils may be supported by speech and language therapists, occupational therapists, educational psychologists and CAMHS, with therapeutic practice built into the school day.

Trained first aiders are planned to be on site during school hours, with first aid kits and a designated first aid area. Individual healthcare plans support pupils with medical conditions such as asthma, diabetes, epilepsy and allergies. Medication is given only with written parental consent. For serious injuries, an ambulance is called.

To confirm before launch: first aider names and the Administration of Medicines Policy link.

Behaviour & positive support

We understand behaviour as communication. Our approach is relationship-based, trauma-informed and restorative. Staff training includes de-escalation, co-regulation and Team Teach. Physical intervention is a last resort, used only to keep someone safe, and parents and carers are told promptly if it has been used. Alder Grove does not use seclusion.

Online & personal safety

Pupils learn about healthy relationships, consent, personal safety and staying safe online through RSE and PSHE. This includes digital footprints, online bullying, gaming risks and safe use of AI tools. Internet access in school is filtered, supervised by trained staff and restricted to approved sites. Pupils are taught where to get help online, including Childline and CEOP.

Written permission is sought before a child’s image is used. Children’s full names and personal details are not published on this website.

Working together

Email is the main way to share newsletters, reports and policy updates; text messages are used for urgent operational news; and phone calls are used for urgent welfare or safeguarding matters.

If something is not resolved, the route is: class teacher or key contact, pastoral lead, senior leadership team, Headteacher, then proprietor/governors. The complaints procedure has three stages: informal, formal in writing to the Headteacher, and a panel hearing.

Looked-after children

Alder Grove admits children who are looked after by a local authority. A Designated Teacher for looked-after and previously looked-after children will work with the child’s Virtual School Head.
