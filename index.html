A different kind of school

A place to be yourself.

Alder Grove is an independent specialist secondary school in Bolton for autistic young people aged 11 to 16 with Education, Health and Care Plans. Many pupils may also have social, emotional and mental health needs, or speech, language and communication needs.

Our approach

Different ways to learn. Room to thrive.

Our curriculum offers academic, vocational and personalised pathways. It combines small-group learning with therapeutic practice and meaningful experiences in the classroom and community.

  • Learning shaped around each pupil’s EHCP
  • Reading, communication and maths prioritised
  • Creative, practical, outdoor and community learning
  • Preparation for adulthood and life beyond Year 11
