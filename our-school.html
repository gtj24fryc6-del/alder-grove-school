Welcome to Alder Grove

Our curriculum puts reading, communication, maths and preparation for adulthood first. It is built around each pupil’s EHCP, taught in small groups, and supported by therapeutic practice so that pupils feel regulated and ready to learn.

Pupils work towards GCSE, Functional Skills, Entry Level and BTEC qualifications, and enjoy a wider curriculum of creative learning, outdoor education, community visits and careers experiences.

Working together

We work in partnership with families, placing local authorities and health and care professionals, including speech and language therapists, occupational therapists, educational psychologists, CAMHS, school nursing and social care. Staff training includes autism practice, trauma-informed and attachment-aware approaches, Team Teach, positive behaviour support, and sensory and communication strategies.

One school, one community

Alder Grove is planned as a single-site school at 49 Bradford Street, Bolton, BL2 1HT. Whatever a young person’s starting point, we believe they can make real progress. Our job is to find the right way in for each pupil and celebrate every step forward.
