Ofsted

Alder Grove is described in the supplied content map as a new independent school that has not yet been inspected. The latest inspection report will be linked here when available. Confirm the school's registration and inspection status before launch.

SEND Information Report

The annual SEND Information Report should explain the types of SEND supported, how needs are identified, interventions, specialist staffing, accessibility and complaints arrangements.

To confirm before launch: upload the current SEND Information Report and Accessibility Plan PDFs; decide which staff-facing documents should be public; resolve the duplicate Safeguarding & Child Protection Policy and Child Protection Policy; add the required complaints data for the previous school year.

Complaints information

The planned complaints process has three stages: informal resolution, a formal complaint in writing to the Headteacher, and a panel hearing. The school must add the required number of formal complaints (Stage 2 and above) from the previous school year before publishing.
