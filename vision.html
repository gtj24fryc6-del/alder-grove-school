Alder Grove School

Our Vision

Enabling every young person at Alder Grove to thrive — academically, socially, emotionally and in everyday life.

Our mission

Young people learn best when they feel safe.

We believe young people learn best when they feel safe, connected to trusted adults, calm, respected and successful.

01

Teamwork

Learning to listen, take turns and solve problems together, with support.

02

High Aspirations

Believing every pupil can make progress, whatever their starting point.

03

Acceptance

Valuing difference, including neurodiversity, culture and identity.

04

Respect

For ourselves, each other and our school, taught and practised every day.

05

Creativity

Finding many ways to express ideas and show what we know.

Our aim

We want every young person to make progress in ways that matter to them: in learning, communication, independence, emotional regulation, friendships and resilience.

An independent specialist secondary school for young people aged 11–16 in Bolton.
